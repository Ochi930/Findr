# Findr Privacy Notice - DRAFT for legal review

*Not legal advice. Have an advocate familiar with the Data Protection Act, 2019
review this before launch.*

## Who we are
Findr is operated by [OPERATOR NAME], registered with the Office of the Data
Protection Commissioner (ODPC) as a data controller/processor [REG NO. - pending].
Contact our data protection officer: [EMAIL].

## What we collect and why
- **Report details** (names, age, description, photos, last-seen information) so
  the public and police can find a missing or found person or pet.
- **Reporter contact details** so police can reach you. These are never shown
  publicly.
- **Tips**. You can send a tip without giving any identity. If you give contact
  details, only police see them. We store a one-way hash of your network address
  to limit abuse, not the address itself.
- **Photos and video**. We only run face or image matching on images where the
  reporter or an authorised officer has given consent for matching.
- **Health or identifying details** on unidentified patients and deceased
  persons, posted by verified hospitals and mortuaries.

## Who sees it
Public listings show a limited set of details. Police officers at the assigned
station and their supervisors can see full details. Every police access to
restricted data is logged.

## How long we keep it
Open cases are kept while active. After a case closes, personal details are
removed or anonymised after [X months - to be decided with legal advice]. Tips
and unmatched images are deleted after [X months].

## Your rights
You may ask to see, correct, restrict or delete your information, and object to
processing, as provided in the Data Protection Act, 2019. Contact [EMAIL]. You
may also complain to the ODPC.

Reports about children are treated as higher priority and shown with fewer
